What I found

Four things standing between you and the phone.

None of this is anyone’s fault. It’s what happens when a site gets built on a template. The settings that slow it down are on by default, and nobody ever turns them off.

19 sec

People leave before the page finishes

The big photo at the top of your homepage takes about 19 seconds to fully load on a phone. Google’s own cutoff is 2.5 seconds. A lot of people are gone before they ever scroll far enough to find your number.

14 screens

There’s no way to call from the middle

Your homepage runs about 14 phone screens long. Someone can tap to call at the very top and again at the very bottom, but nothing in between. A guy reading about Botox halfway down has to scroll all the way back up to reach you.

9 of 10

Your booking buttons don’t book

Ten buttons on your homepage say BOOK. Only one of them actually opens your Vagaro booking. The other nine drop people on a page with no booking and no phone number in sight.

0 words

Google and AI can’t read your address

Your address only exists inside a picture of a map. Google and tools like ChatGPT read the words on a page, not the pictures. So when someone searches for a clinic near them, you’re harder to find than you should be.
