Explains a lot

Critical

Before we start

Two things to flag

Cold outreach is a legal risk

  • Texting or calling skip-traced numbers falls under the TCPA.
  • Penalties run $500 to $1,500 per message.
  • You said yourself about 70% of a list is on the do-not-call registry.
  • Scrubbing is built into Phase Three. I won't skip it.

Blue-bubble texting is unstable

  • Project Blue and tools like it run against Apple's terms.
  • They get shut down every so often.
  • I'll build it so you can swap providers without redoing your outbound.
